Seven arms fine-tune the upstream 1.5B-step policy on the deploy arena (upstream rewards, DR and network, clip 0.3): five ACCEL variants and two DR controls, 300M env steps each, B also continued to 1B. Every policy, the base included, ran the same eval_brax levels and reset keys on warp: 2048 levels × 4 episodes × 2 passes, flat on 1024 levels.

Win criterion, fixed before running

  • Primary: random/d1.0/cmd_success_rate and random/d1.0/success_rate, 3-seed mean above the base with the 95% CIs (levels × seeds bootstrap) not overlapping the base's (levels bootstrap).
  • Guards: no drop > 2 points on flat and d0.25 success; d1.0 fall rate not worse (paired difference not significantly above 0).
  • Only ACCEL arms can be the uploaded policy; DR arms are controls.
  • Secondary: v10box d1.0 success, suite success.

Why plain ACCEL did not move terrain success

  • With the easy generator (control points within generator_scale 0.1 of flat) and 2 edits of ±0.1, the buffer stays next to flat: genome distance 0.045–0.050, training success 0.72–0.73, while d1.0 eval success is ~0.32. Even 1B steps only reach 0.076.
  • The DR-mixed generator plus growing mutations (D, F) put the buffer at 0.25–0.27, training success ~0.5. SFL's p(1−p) score keeps the levels where the student succeeds about half the time.
  • At lr 3e-4 every fine-tune lost ground first (A; C's command tracking). At lr 1e-4 nothing regressed.
