Reading the result

  • No arm beats the base under the pre-registered criterion, per seed or pooled. Nothing is exported: the policy to upload stays the base, which is not ours.
  • B (ACCEL, lr 1e-4) is the only arm that clears a primary metric: d1.0 command-conditioned success +2.7 points [+2.1, +3.4], all three seeds 44.0–44.8 vs 41.6, CIs apart. d1.0 success is +0.8 [+0.0, +1.6], CIs overlapping, so it fails the second primary metric. v10box: +1.7 success, +2.3 cmd.
  • A (T6's flags, lr 3e-4) is worse than the base everywhere: d1.0 cmd −1.1, success −1.6, v10box success −3.0, suite −5.0. The only change from A to B is the learning rate, so 3e-4 is too high for a fine-tune of this policy.
  • C (DR, lr 3e-4) wins on terrain, not on tracking: d1.0 fall rate −9.3 points, success +1.3 [+0.6, +2.0], v10box success +6.8, cmd unchanged (+0.1). DR trains on the eval distribution itself.
  • Flat guard: every arm reads 3–5 points below the base on flat (base 75.0 [70.9, 79.1], 64 levels). The set is too small to resolve a 2-point guard; d0.25 (2048 levels) drops 1.6–2.3 points, A fails it.

Why ACCEL does not move terrain success

  • ACCEL's buffer stays next to flat. Second half of training: mean genome distance from flat 0.045 (A) / 0.050 (B) vs DR's 0.345; training success 0.73 / 0.72 vs DR 0.29, while d1.0 eval success is ~0.32.
  • The easy generator (control points within generator_scale 0.1 of flat) plus 2 edits of ±0.1 never reaches d1.0 terrain in 300M steps. B's gain is in command tracking on easy terrain, which is what it trained on.
  • In-training eval vs the base: A stays at or below it (success −2 to −3 points from 50M on). B's cmd success rises after ~150M (+4 to +5 points at 200–250M, +2.5 at 300M); its success ends +1 to +2. C's success sits 1–2 points above from ~150M, its cmd at the base.

T6 seed 2 collapse and launch 1

  • Both are one mechanism. MJX blows up on single envs now and then: on warp ~1–5 env steps per 2.46M-step iteration (DR: 92% of iterations; ACCEL: 7%). The blown-up obs reach brax's running obs normalizer. The base carries a count of 1.5e9, so the poisoned variance never washes out.
  • deploy_ws_accel_seed2 (T6, jax): iteration 55 healthy (reward 0.0515, train success 0.73, falls 8e-5); iteration 56 nan in reward, travel and eval. T6's report places the nan in iteration 55's PPO update. Iteration 56 still logged a normal fall rate (7.2e-5 per step; 0 from iteration 57 on, when nan actions stop every fall), so its rollout ran on finite params for most of the iteration and went nan inside it. That fits an env blow-up reaching the normalizer and does not fit params that were nan from the start of iteration 56. The run kept no per-update losses, so an update-side nan inside iteration 56 is not excluded; both guards now exist.
  • Launch 1 (code 0fb3708) already ended nan steps, but not the exploding finite steps before them. A seed 1 and all three C seeds collapsed within one iteration of a blow-up (dr seed 0: mean forward velocity 96 m/s in that iteration). Obs std vs the base normalizer, all in joint-velocity dims: ×1.04 in a healthy run, ×2045 (A seed 1), ×640753 (C seed 1). Launch 1 was cancelled at 50–150M.
  • Fix (launch 2, b84a645): FunctionalEnv ends any step with a non-finite state or |qvel| > 200 (random actions peak at ~75); brax_ued stops if any obs std leaves 0.1–10× of the warm start's (launch 2 stayed within 0.96–1.20); PPO losses and grad norm are logged. Training-only: the deploy snapshot test passes unchanged.

Proposals

Cost per 300M-step run, measured: 0.47 GPU-h training + 0.31 GPU-h for both final evals.

  • 1. Harder ACCEL at lr 1e-4, with its matched DR control. --generator mixed --mutation grow (fresh levels from DR-mixed, edits grow roughness), lr 1e-4, plus DR at lr 1e-4 (C ran at 3e-4, so B vs C mixes curriculum and learning rate). 6 runs × 300M: ~4.7 GPU-h. Recommended first.
  • 2. SFL scoring. Proposal 1 with --score learnability (levels where p(1−p) peaks, the closest score to the success metric). 3 runs: ~2.4 GPU-h.
  • 3. Continue B to 1B. --init_from each B seed's 300M checkpoint for 700M more (fresh Adam state and buffer). Tests whether the tracking gain grows and success follows. 3 runs: ~3.6 GPU-h.
  • Not needed: freezing the imported normalizer (the divergence guard holds it within 1.2×).
