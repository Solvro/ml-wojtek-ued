ACCEL fine-tune vs the 1.5B base

Three arms fine-tune the upstream 1.5B-step policy for 300M env steps on the deploy arena (upstream rewards, DR and network, clip 0.3), 3 seeds each. Every policy, the base included, ran the same eval_brax levels and reset keys on warp: 2048 levels × 4 episodes × 2 passes.

Win criterion, fixed before running

  • Primary: random/d1.0/cmd_success_rate and random/d1.0/success_rate, 3-seed mean above the base with the 95% CIs (levels × seeds bootstrap) not overlapping the base's (levels bootstrap).
  • Guards: no drop > 2 points on flat and d0.25 success; d1.0 fall rate not worse (paired difference not significantly above 0).
  • Secondary: v10box d1.0 success, suite success.
