## Recommendation

- **(a) Pilot-only, within days:** [SimBench2Real](https://corl2026-realworld-robot-learning.github.io/) (Oct 14, 11:59 UTC on OpenReview, 5 pp) or the SRF late-breaking track (Oct 15 23:59 CDT, 4 pp). Low cost, non-archival (SRF) and a direct topic match. Caveats:
  - In-person in Austin on Nov 12 (travel/visa/cost from PL); neither page says a presenter must attend, but both are in-person-only events and CoRL 2026 sets no central remote option — ask organizers if no one can travel.
  - SimBench2Real archival status is not stated — confirm with organizers before submitting if a later full version matters.
  - Submit to **one** CoRL workshop only: both are on Nov 12, the same day.
- **(b) With two-robot v10 results:**
  - If v10 lands by **Oct 15**: [SRF @ CoRL 2026](https://sim2real2field-corl26.github.io/), Late-breaking track (≤4 pp, non-archival; the 8 pp Published track requires prior acceptance elsewhere). Best fit of all open calls (environment design for transfer, tail-risk evaluation; the physical Wojtek robot supports the "real" story even without sim2real numbers yet). SimBench2Real (≤5 pp) is the alternative if the extra page matters.
  - If v10 slips past Oct 15: no good workshop until **ICLR 2027 workshops (~Feb 1, 2027)**; check the list on Nov 29, 2026 for an open-endedness / RL / embodied workshop.
- **(c) Backup / conference version:**
  - [RSS 2027](https://roboticsconference.org/information/cfp/) extended abstract by Dec 4, 2026 — archival, but stage 1 suits a benchmark with pilot + planned extensions; doubles as the "full conference version".
  - ICRA 2027 workshops (calls ~Feb–Apr 2027) for the sim2real follow-up.
  - Archival without deadline pressure: TMLR; NeurIPS 2027 E&D (~May 2027, TBA).
  - Skip AAAI-27 workshops (no topic fit) and L4DC (archival, control-theory audience, deadline Nov 13).
